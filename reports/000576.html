What to watch for

Electrode types and counts: only in the paper.

The paper says: Depth electrodes (AdTech, 8 contacts each) in amygdala, each with nine microelectrodes. The data doesn't state it, so treat this as unconfirmed. Grid, strip and depth contacts record different things; mixing them blindly biases any model.

“Each mac- roelectrode had nine microelectrodes that protruded approximately 4 mm from its tip (Fig. 1b).” · 10.1038/s41597-020-00790-x
Electrode localization method: only in the paper.

The paper says: Postimplantation CT registered to postimplantation T1 MRI in FieldTrip (ACPC), contacts visually marked, normalized to MNI space, labeled with Brainnetome; verified by neurosurgeon. The data doesn't state it, so treat this as unconfirmed. You can't place contacts reliably in a shared brain space, so cross-patient analysis is limited.

“The contact positions were normalized to the MNI space and assigned to a brain region using Brainnetome23.” · 10.1038/s41597-020-00790-x
Subjects by diagnosis: only in the paper.

The paper says: 9 patients with drug-resistant focal epilepsy. The data doesn't state it, so treat this as unconfirmed. You can't tell which patient population a model would be learning from.

“All participants were patients with drug-resistant focal epilepsy.” · 10.1038/s41597-020-00790-x
Consent scope: only in the paper.

The paper says: Written informed consent to participate in the study. The data doesn't state it, so treat this as unconfirmed. It's unclear whether reuse, especially commercial reuse, is covered by what patients agreed to.

“All participants provided written informed consent to participate in the study.” · 10.1038/s41597-020-00790-x
Ethics approval: only in the paper.

The paper says: Kantonale Ethikkommission Zürich, PB-2016-02055. The data doesn't state it, so treat this as unconfirmed. There is no record of ethics approval to point to if your use is questioned.

“The study was approved by the institutional ethics review board (Kantonale Ethikkommission Zürich, PB-2016-02055).” · 10.1038/s41597-020-00790-x

How we checked

We read the dataset's descriptive files (sidecars, channel and electrode tables, NWB headers) without downloading the recordings, and the open-access paper(s) describing it (10.1038/s41597-020-00790-x). Values from a paper are only used when backed by a sentence quoted word for word from it, and they stay labelled as unconfirmed. We check that information is present and consistent; we don't judge the science. Paywalled papers are not read.

brain-bench grades are independent: whoever pays for a report has no say in its result. This report refers to the dataset version above.
