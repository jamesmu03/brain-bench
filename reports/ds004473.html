What to watch for

Recording system / amplifier: missing.

Neither the data nor an open-access paper states it. You can't correct for hardware differences when combining this with data from other hospitals.

Reference scheme: missing.

Neither the data nor an open-access paper states it. The same activity looks different under different references, so signals may not be comparable to other datasets.

Subjects by diagnosis: only in the paper.

The paper says: 8 patients with medically intractable focal epilepsy. The data doesn't state it, so treat this as unconfirmed. You can't tell which patient population a model would be learning from.

“Eight patients with medically intractable focal epilepsy were surgically implanted with sEEG electrodes for clinical localization of ictal (seizure) onset and epileptogenic zones.” · 10.1088/1741-2552/acae0a
Task design: only in the paper.

The paper says: Forced two-choice reaction time task: left/right arrow cue after 300-700 ms fixation, respond with arrow keys; 300 trials. The data doesn't state it, so treat this as unconfirmed. Labels are hard to interpret without knowing what patients were doing.

“Patients performed a forced two-choice reaction time task with the left and right arrow keys on a laptop using the index and middle finger of their dominant hand respectively ( Figure 1 ).” · 10.1088/1741-2552/acae0a
Consent scope: only in the paper.

The paper says: Written informed consent, including publication of anonymized identifiable data. The data doesn't state it, so treat this as unconfirmed. It's unclear whether reuse, especially commercial reuse, is covered by what patients agreed to.

“Consent was given for publication of anonymized identifiable data from all participants.” · 10.1088/1741-2552/acae0a
Ethics approval: only in the paper.

The paper says: Oregon Health & Science University IRB (MOD00042329). The data doesn't state it, so treat this as unconfirmed. There is no record of ethics approval to point to if your use is questioned.

“IRB approval was obtained at Oregon Health & Science University (MOD00042329).” · 10.1088/1741-2552/acae0a

How we checked

We read the dataset's descriptive files (sidecars, channel and electrode tables, NWB headers) without downloading the recordings, and the open-access paper(s) describing it (10.1088/1741-2552/acae0a). Values from a paper are only used when backed by a sentence quoted word for word from it, and they stay labelled as unconfirmed. We check that information is present and consistent; we don't judge the science. Paywalled papers are not read.
